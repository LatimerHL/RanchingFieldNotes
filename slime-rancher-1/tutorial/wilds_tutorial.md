*Survival guide to The Wilds.*

## 任务说明
荒蛮群岛上到处都能找到库卡多巴果，剑齿史莱姆的泥球里面也会包着库卡多巴果。

把泥球带到库卡粉碎机那里打碎，就能得到里面的库卡多巴果了。

通往荒蛮群岛的传送器有点不太靠谱。每次传送不一定都能到达同样的地点，而且你还需要找到另一个传送器回到奥格登的别墅。

**原文参考：**

> Kookadobas can be found growing all over The Wilds or packed together in saber slime mudballs.
> 
>   Bring mudballs to kookacrackers to release the kookadobas inside.
> 
>   Teleporters to The Wilds are a little unreliable.  You won't always end up in the same spot each time and you'll need to find a different teleporter back to Ogden's Retreat.
