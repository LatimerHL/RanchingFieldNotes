## 关于
非常母鸡是为了适应致远牧星环境而培育的特殊品种。虽说看起来和平常的鸡没什么两样，这些母鸡却十分擅长照料自己，因此深受平时要面对数百只饥饿史莱姆，分身乏术的牧场主们的青睐。

**原文参考：**

> Hen hens are a special breed of chicken bred to survive the climate of the Far, Far Range.     While appearing more or less like a big chicken, these burly birds are much more adept at fending for themselves,     making them ideal for ranchers who might otherwise be distracted by a few hundred hungry slimes.

## 在牧场
公鸡周围的非常母鸡每隔一段时间就会下蛋，并孵出鸡仔儿。然而，如果母鸡或公鸡密度过大，就会导致它们感到焦虑，停止产蛋。能够理解鸡与鸡之间恋爱结合的复杂过程的聪明牧场主们，从来都会注意不在鸡圈里放超过12只成年公鸡母鸡。

**原文参考：**

> Hen hens in close proximity to roostros will periodically lay eggs that produce chickadoos.     However, keeping too many hens or roostros in close proximity makes them anxious and egg production will come     to a halt. Savvy ranchers with an understanding of the complex nature of chicken romance always keep their     coops from exceeding 12 grown chickens.
