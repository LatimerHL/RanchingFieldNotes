## 关于
丝滑细沙是一种玻璃荒漠中独有的常见资源，可用泵机获取。

尽管玻璃荒漠被沙子覆盖，丝滑细沙则是在其表面下被发现的。它是如此柔滑纯粹，摸起来就像水一样。它的这种特性非常适于装置制造。

**原文参考：**

> Silky sand is a common resource exclusive to the Glass Desert, obtained with a pump.
>     
>     Though the Glass Desert is covered with sand, silky sand found deep below the surface is so smooth and pure it 	almost feels like water. These qualities make it very useful in gadget fabrication.

## 在牧场
将丝滑细沙放入实验室的精炼炉，就可以用它制作史莱姆科技装置。

**原文参考：**

> Deposit silky sand into the Lab's Refinery where they can then be used for fabricating     Slime Science gadgets.
