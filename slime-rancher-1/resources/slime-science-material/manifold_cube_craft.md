## 关于
多重立方体是史莱姆科学家维克多·亨福里为了提升工作效率而发明出来的。多重立方体中包含上千种稀有矿物和复合物，通过制造器可以将它完全转换成所需的物质。

莫奇曾经想花重金向维克多收购多重立方体的配方，但他委婉谢绝了。之后，有迹象表明维克多的数据库曾多次遭受攻击。当他就此事询问莫奇的时候，莫奇委婉地否定了自己的嫌疑。

**原文参考：**

> A manifold cube is an invention of slime scientist Viktor Humphries. He developed manifold cubes in order to save time, as manifold cubes contain traces of thousands of unique minerals and compounds, one of which can be activated by a fabricator to transform the whole manifold cube into the selected material.
> 
>   Mochi once offered Viktor a fortune in newbucks for the formula to creating manifold cubes, and he politely declined. Following this, Viktor noted several attempts to hack his data server. When Viktor asked Mochi to comment on the incident, she politely declined.
