## 习性
喵喵史莱姆和地球上驯养的家猫有着许多惊人的相似之处。尖尖的耳朵，后背的条纹，还有摇晃的尾巴......要不是它们软弹弹的，跟真的猫没什么两样呢。

**原文参考：**

> Tabby slimes share many curious similarities to domesticated house cats of Earth. With their perky ears, striped backs, and swaying tails, you'd almost mistake them for a true feline... if they weren't made entirely of gooey slime, of course.

## 注意事项
喵喵史莱姆通常不会对牧场主造成威胁。但正如它们那些八竿子打不着的亲戚——地球家猫那样，喵喵史莱姆经常会造成大混乱。喵喵史莱姆喜欢偷藏自己不吃的东西，比如水果和蔬菜等，经常导致致远牧星上意外生成各种大型史莱姆。

**原文参考：**

> A tabby slime generally doesn't mean any harm to a rancher, but like their spiritual Earth cat cousins, often causes a great deal of chaos anyway. Tabby slimes love to steal things they don't eat, like fruits and veggies, resulting in lots of unintended largo transformations on the Range.

**出现地点**：彩虹原野 灰烬山谷 灰色迷宫

**性格**：温顺（Docile）

**行为**（译自 fandom）：不饿或吃不了的食物会被偷走并试图放到别处；叼起食物时会喵喵叫；跳跃力高于多数史莱姆；偶尔会跟着玩家；偶尔扑击玩家（扑击前会扭动）

> 原文：Steals Food when not hungry or that it cannot eat and attempts to place it elsewhere. ；Meows when picking up food. ；Can jump higher than most Slimes. ；Will occasionally follow the player. ；Occasionally attempts to pounce at the player, indicated by wiggling before making a jump in her direction.
