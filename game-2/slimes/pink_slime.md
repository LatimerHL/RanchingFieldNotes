## 习性
粉红史莱姆是致远牧星上最为常见的史莱姆。它们十分温驯活泼，也是最容易圈养的史莱姆。粉红史莱姆完全不挑食，喂什么都吃，但它没有最喜欢的食物，粉红结晶也是所有结晶中价格最便宜的。

**原文参考：**

> Pink slimes are the most common slime found on the Far, Far Range. They’re cheerful, docile, and the easiest of all slimes to ranch. A pink slime will eat anything you put in front of them, though they have no favorite food, and their plorts are the least valuable of all slimes.

## 注意事项
粉红史莱姆并没什么让人操心的地方。其软乎乎弹嘟嘟，很喜欢与牧场主接触，在致远牧星随处可见。但也正因为如此，牧场附近到处都能看到粉红结晶，随之而来的就是杂交出大型史莱姆，然后就是......

**原文参考：**

> Not much to worry about with a pink slime. They're soft, squishy, very happy to be around a rancher, and found all over the Range. But because of this, a rancher won't go far without finding a few pink plorts, which can lead to largos, and then...

## 结晶说明
粉红结晶是一种极佳的“多用途常见物质”，从家用清洁剂到食品，都能见到它的影子。在地球的人们，平时吃的汉堡，给咖啡加的糖精，哪怕擦地时用的洗洁剂都是粉红结晶的产物。简直太万能了！

**原文参考：**

> Pink plorts are an excellent 'multipurpose generic substance' used to manufacture everything from food products to household cleaners. All over earth, people are eating burgers, sweetening their coffees, or scrubbing their floors with pink plort products. That's some serious versatility!
