## 习性
浣熊史莱姆是麻烦制造者，趁着夜色小偷小摸是它们最大的爱好，而在太阳升起前满载而归最让它们开心。浣熊史莱姆之所以只在月光下犯事作恶是因为它们有个特殊情况——暴露在阳光下时就会变成石头。虽说这并不是一种古老的诅咒，但它们就是那种该受到古老诅咒的史莱姆。

**原文参考：**

> Ringtail slimes are nothing but trouble. Theft under cover of darkness is their favorite hobby and they delight in getting away with as much as possible before the sun rises. The reason for this exclusively moonlit crime spree is that ringtails have a peculiar condition that transforms them into stone when exposed to sunlight. And while we're not saying this is an ancient curse, they're definitely the type of slime who would have an ancient curse if that was a thing.

## 注意事项
浣熊史莱姆一旦开吃，就永远停不下来。只要时间充足，一只浣熊史莱姆就能把整个牧场吃个干干净净。而且它们不会提供任何回报，因为浣熊史莱姆吃饱以后就会停止生产结晶。如果你打算牧养这些贪婪的小无赖，记得留意它们脸上淘气的小表情，这样就能知道什么时候该停止喂它们了......

**原文参考：**

> Once a ringtail slime starts eating, it will never stop. Just one ringtail slime is capable of wiping out an entire farm if given the time. And they offer nothing in return because a ringtail slime stops producing plorts when it would otherwise be full. If you plan on ranching these ravenous rascals, keep an eye out for a mischievous expression to know when to avoid feeding them...

## 结晶说明
从浣熊结晶开发出的材料具备极强的弹性，而在受到紫外线照射后会变硬。浣熊结晶和粉红结晶很相像，有着很强的延展性。善于利用的人可以拿它做出各种各样经得起时间考验的东西，这些东西可以是生活中的必需品，比如偏远岛屿上的坚固庇护所，或是一本即使在灼热沙漠中跋涉也不会损坏的家人相册集......

**原文参考：**

> Materials developed from ringtail plorts are extremely resilient, actually hardening in the face of UV rays. And similar to pink plorts, ringtail plorts are very malleable, allowing a clever person to craft a wide range of things that can stand the test of time. Such things could be something as essential as sturdy shelter on a faraway island, or as simple as a collection of family photos that could survive a trek across a scorching desert...
