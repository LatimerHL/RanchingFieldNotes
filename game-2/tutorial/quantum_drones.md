## 内容

### 无人机部署（Placing Drones）

进入装置模式放置无人机站点装置。部署无人机站点时会同步部署停靠的无人机。

**原文参考：**

> Enter Gadget Mode to place Drone Station gadgets. When you place a Drone Station, you also place the drone that comes with it, as they will be docked to their Drone Stations.

### 无人机模式（Drone Modes）

<b>牧场主无人机</b> - 把无人机站点装置放在温室或它的扩展区，让无人机在牧场主模式下行动。

**原文参考：**

> <b>Rancher Drones</b> - Place Drone Station gadgets in the Conservatory or its expansions to have the drones act in Rancher mode.

### 无人机模式（Drone Modes）

<b>勘探无人机</b> - 把无人机站点装置放在温室外面，让无人机在勘探模式下行动。在勘探模式下，无人机收集的结晶和资源会被发送到量子云。

**原文参考：**

> <b>Explorer Drones</b> - Place Drone Station gadgets outside of the Conservatory to have the drones act in Explorer mode. Drones in Explorer mode gather plorts and resources which are sent to the Quantum Cloud.

### 无人机编程（Drone Programming）

通过访问其无人机站点装置的菜单，对量子无人机进行编程，使其执行任务。你可以更改它的<b>目标</b>（正在采集的东西）、<b>来源</b>（前去采集的位置）和<b>目的地</b>（采集物的送达地点）。完成设置后，<b>激活你的无人机</b>让它开始任务。

**原文参考：**

> Program your Quantum Drone to do tasks by accessing the menu from its Drone Station gadget. You can change its <b>Target</b> (what it's collecting), <b>Source</b> (where it's looking), and <b>Destination</b> (where it takes the things it collects). Once you’re done, <b>Activate Your Drone</b> to get it started on its tasks.

### 无人机燃料补充（Fueling Drones）

随时间消耗能量的无人机可通过向<b>无人机站点</b>发射结晶进行充能（无论是否停靠！）。你甚至可以进行超额充能至100%以上，从而减少充能次数。

**原文参考：**

> Drones lose energy over time. Refuel by shooting plorts into a <b>Drone Station</b>—whether it's docked or not! You can even overcharge it past 100% to refuel less often.

### 利用无人机放牧（Ranching with Drones）

使用结晶收集器可以保证无人机进行高效拾取作业。

想用无人机向牧栏运送蔬菜、水果和肉类吗？先把你想要的食物放进自动喂食器里，无人机就会自动补充食物！

**原文参考：**

> Use plort collectors to guarantee an efficient drone pickup.

> Setting a drone to deliver veggies, fruits, and meat to corrals? Put your desired food in the auto feeder first, and the drone will keep it stocked!

## 相关装置

**无人机（Drone）**：编程后，可以在你的牧场执行多种任务的辅助机器人。

> A helper bot that can be programmed to buzz around your ranch and perform a variety of tasks.

**高级无人机（Advanced Drone）**：更高级版本的无人机，能够同时执行两个任务。

> ⚠️ **2026-08-18 修正：高级无人机（Advanced Drone）仅一代实装**（维克多工作间 二阶段 交易，1.4.0 引入）；**二代只有量子无人机一种，无普通/高级之分**，此条不属于二代装置。

> A more advanced version of a drone that has the capacity to perform two tasks at once.

**量子无人机站点（Quantum Drone Station）**：量子无人机能穿透障碍物闪烁移动，并将物品存储在量子云中。它们在牧场和探索致远牧星时会执行不同任务。记得给它们充能以获得最佳效果！

> Quantum Drones flicker through obstacles and store items in the quantum cloud. They do different tasks if at the ranch or out on the range. Charge them up for best results!
