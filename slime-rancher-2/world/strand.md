# 星光滨岸（Starlight Strand）

## 名称
星光滨岸（Starlight Strand）

**原文参考：**

> Starlight Strand

## 简介
黄昏与黎明合而为一的景象。

**原文参考：**

> A vision of dusk and dawn together as one.

## 内容
星光滨岸是一个完全相反的世界：蜿蜒曲折的土地被史莱姆之海不规则地切割，而高耸的高地与悬崖使得低处的湿地若隐若现。

而且，一切都在珊瑚和薰衣草这两个相对的色调之间神秘地分开。当你从一边穿行向另一边时，氛围也会发生变化。

星光滨岸是一片长满植物的幻境，使得这里成为热爱花蜜的蜂蜜史莱姆和噗啦蝶史莱姆的理想家园。树冠间蜂群们忙忙碌碌，能在这里收获天然蜂蜜和嗡嗡蜂蜡。

虽然不可否认这里是个美丽无比的地方，但星光滨岸独特的性质不太像是自然产生的，暗示着彩虹岛中心隐藏着秘密的历史。

**原文参考：**

> The Starlight Strand is a world of opposites: a winding land cut erratically by the Slime Sea, with towering heights and cliffs that loom over deep lowland marshes.

> Further, it is all mysteriously split right down the middle between two opposing palettes of coral and lavender, with the very atmosphere itself seeming to change as you cross from one to the other.

> The strand is a floral fantasia, making it an ideal home to nectar-loving honey and flutter slimes, and its canopies are abuzz with hives that can be harvested for wild honey and buzz wax.

> Though undeniably beautiful, the Starlight Strand's peculiar nature is unlikely a natural occurrence, and suggests a secret history hidden within the heart of Rainbow Island.
