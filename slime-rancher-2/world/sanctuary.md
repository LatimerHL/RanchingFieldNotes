## 简介
一个专门为需要额外照顾的史莱姆准备的秘密藏身处......

**原文参考：**

> A secret hideaway for slimes that need a little extra care-taking...

## 内容
圣域既熟悉又陌生：这是一座全新的岛屿，但显然是由建造迷宫的先民开垦而成，岛上还建有一座同样古老、用途同样神秘的设施。

探险家从码头下船后，映入眼帘的是高耸的悬崖，上面刻着美丽而奇异的符号，还有一座巨大的、散发着奇异光芒的史莱姆雕像。

圣域四周柔波环绕，史莱姆之海的波浪轻轻拍打着，弥漫着宁静祥和的气氛，长满苔藓的古老植被让一切都与自然完美融合。

这里更大的秘密就隐藏在其中，牧场主的相关线索暗示，这里或许是用来照料史莱姆的地方，而在深处，还会遇见一位与众不同的新朋友......

**原文参考：**

> The Sanctuary is familiar and unfamiliar at the same time: a brand new island, but one clearly cultivated by the people of the past. Whoever created the Labyrinth has built here an equally ancient facility for an equally mysterious purpose.

> On disembarking from the docks, an explorer is greeted with towering cliffs with beautiful yet strange symbols marked on them, and an enormous, oddly radiant slime statue.

> Surrounded by the gently lapping waves of the Slime Sea, a sense of peace permeates the Sanctuary, and mossy old growth makes everything feel perfectly in tune with nature.

> Its greater secrets lie within, with plots for ranchers implying that this place may be used to care for slimes, and an unusual new friend to meet inside...
