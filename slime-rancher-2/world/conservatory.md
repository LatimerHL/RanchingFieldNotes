## 内容
碧翠丝刚到达彩虹岛的时候，她发现这座神秘的废弃温室，并决定把它当做自己在新世界的家园。这里具备熟练农场主开展牧场生活所需要的一切设施。

温室是岛上唯一一座貌似近期才建造的建筑，和遍布在彩虹岛以及致远牧星其他地方的古代史莱姆遗迹形成了鲜明的对比。

奇怪的是，它各种结构上的缺陷和磨损似乎是设计的一部分，就好像是为博物馆展览而设计的。然而给人最深的感觉还是，这里曾是某人的家，是倾注了爱和意志而建造的。

**原文参考：**

> When Beatrix first arrived at Rainbow Island she discovered this mysterious, abandoned Conservatory and decided to use it as her home away from home, as it has all the facilities an experienced slime rancher would need to make a living.

> The Conservatory itself is the only structure on the island that appears to have been constructed recently, standing in stark contrast to the ancient slime ruins found all over Rainbow Island and the rest of the Far, Far Range.

> Strangely, imperfections and wear on its various structures seem to be part of the design, as if it was created for a museum exhibit. And yet, there is a palpable feeling throughout that this was a home to someone, that it was built with love and purpose.
