## 简介
大片的水与史莱姆合成物形成的海洋，不适合游泳，更不要说饮用了。

**原文参考：**

> An expansive body of water and slime compound that's not fit for swimming, let alone drinking.

## 内容
神秘的史莱姆之海覆盖着致远牧星表面的绝大部分面积。目前看来，它是水和史莱姆的混合体，据说是史莱姆最早的原始生命之源。不过它真正作用，就连最顶尖的科学家也完全摸不着头脑。

目前已知的是史莱姆之海中获取的液体不可饮用，不可用于灌溉，连在里面游泳也不行，因为无论什么东西，一旦进去就会迅速沉底。

史莱姆掉入史莱姆之海会迅速消失，但大家都认为这并不代表这个史莱姆的终结，因为经常可以看到掉到史莱姆之海的史莱姆很快重新出现在陆地上，而且依然心情好好的样子。

实际上，许多史莱姆科学家都认为史莱姆把史莱姆之海当做一种传送的手段，它们可以快速在水面下移动，然后随心出现在陆地之上。

但不论如何，有一件事是不容置疑的，对牧场主来说尤其如此：不要进入史莱姆之海！

**原文参考：**

> The Slime Sea that covers most of the Far, Far Range is something of a mystery. It is seemingly a mixture of water and slime that is believed to be the primordial pool whence all slimes emerged, though its true purpose is entirely unknown by even the most esteemed slime scientists.

> What is known is that the liquid found within the Slime Sea is not fit for drinking, watering crops, or even swimming, as most things seem to quickly sink to the bottom.

> When a slime enters the slime sea it quickly vanishes but it is widely believed that this far from ends the slime's existence, for slimes that enter the sea can often be seen quickly reappearing back on land, happy as ever.

> In fact, many slime scientists believe that slimes use the sea as a means of transportation, moving swiftly below the surface and popping out on land wherever they see fit.

> But all theories aside, one fact remains very clear, most especially to ranchers: don't enter the Slime Sea!
