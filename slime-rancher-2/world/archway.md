拱廊（The Archway）

**原文参考：**

## 简介
通往温室的扩张区域，设置在古老废墟和彩虹原野之间。

**原文参考：**

> An expansion to the conservatory set amongst ancient ruins and fields of rainbow grass.

## 内容
拱廊是通往温室的扩张区域，很久很久以前就设置在覆盖着草皮的废墟中。饱饱果和胡萝卜在石块裂缝中生长，对那些喜欢吃水果和蔬菜的史莱姆来说，可是块好地方。

**原文参考：**

> The Archway is an expansion to the conservatory set amongst grass-covered ruins from a time long ago. Pogo fruit and carrots grow between the cracks in the stone, making this a great spot for slimes that enjoy some fruit and veggies in their diet.
