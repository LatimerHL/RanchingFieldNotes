# 新手引导弹窗（Onboarding）

> 来源：游戏内本地化 Tutorial 表（t.* 标题 + m.text.* 引导正文，官中+原文）
> 版本：Slime Rancher 2

游戏内随进度弹出的引导提示，覆盖基础操作、牧场经营、史莱姆科技与剧情关键节点。

## 基础操作

### 牧场主的真空枪教学（How to vac like a rancher）

你的真空枪可以吸取物品并将其储存在真空储槽里。

**原文参考：**

> Your vacpack can vac things up and store them in a vac tank.

### 来跳一下！（Hop to it!）

### 你吸到了一个史莱姆！（You vac'd a slime!）

有了史莱姆，才好搞致富。将史莱姆射向牧栏，它便会穿越障壁进到里面去。

**原文参考：**

> Slimes are key to your success. Shoot slimes THROUGH a corral barrier into the corral on the ranch.

### 获得了可口的食物！（Tasty food acquired!）

像是水果、蔬菜、鸡这些食物，都能拿来喂史莱姆。向粉红史莱姆发射食物看看，它们来者不拒。

**原文参考：**

> Food like fruits, veggies, or chickens are used to feed slimes. Try shooting food at a pink slime; they'll eat anything.

### 获得地图数据！（Map Data Acquired!）

探索牧星上的地图数据点，来丰富你的地图。

**原文参考：**

> Discover map data nodes across the range to uncover more of your map.

### 欢迎回来......（Welcome back...）

强制返回后，至少得休息12小时才能恢复体力。你要是不够小心，醒来之后就得面对一群饿到发脾气的史莱姆哦！

**原文参考：**

> When you're knocked out you'll rest several hours in order to recover. If you're not careful, you might wake up to a mob of hungry, unruly slimes on your ranch!

### 彩虹岛等你去探索！（Rainbow Island awaits!）

基础的部分你都学到了，该出去闯闯了！

记住，无论你将去何方探险，心中都要装着求知欲和善意。

**原文参考：**

> That about covers the basics, now it's time to get out there and explore!

> And no matter where your adventure takes you, always keep curiosity and kindness in your heart.

## 牧场经营

### 你获得了一个史莱姆结晶！（You acquired a slime plort!）

史莱姆吃掉食物就会产出结晶。

快到结晶市场拿结晶换钱吧。

**原文参考：**

> When slimes eat they make plorts.

> Head on over to the Plort Market to cash in your plort.

### 结晶市场（The Plort Market）

在结晶市场，可以用你辛苦获得的史莱姆结晶换新币。

把结晶射入结晶市场，就能换来钱啦！

**原文参考：**

> The Plort Market gives you newbucks for your hard-earned plorts.

> Shoot a plort into the Plort Market and earn some cash!

### 你建造了一个园地！（You built a garden!）

向存放器发射一个水果或者蔬菜，园地里就能长出更多同种蔬果来！

**原文参考：**

> Shoot a fruit or veggie into the depositor to begin growing more of that type!

### 溅点儿水花！（Make a splash!）

用水冲一下，能让任何史莱姆冷静一小会儿，还能用来干掉焦油怪！

浇过水的作物也会长得更快！

**原文参考：**

> A blast of fresh water will calm down any slime for a short while, or dissolve those terrible tarr slimes!

> A watered garden's crops also grow faster!

### 通讯站（The Comm Station）

通讯站现已上线！可联系致远牧星的其他牧场主。他们能提供帮助信息、赠送礼物，又或者只是闲聊畅谈。

**原文参考：**

> The Comm Station is now online! Call other ranchers throughout the Far, Far Range. They might have something for you: helpful info, gifts, or just good conversation.

### 彩虹币？不如叫“采购”币！（Rainbow Coin? More like rainBUY coin!）

## 史莱姆科技

### 精炼炉（The Refinery）

要制造装置，首先要确保精炼炉里有足够的资源。把结晶发射到精炼炉里试试看吧。

不过记住，一旦将资源放入精炼炉，就无法取出了哦！

**原文参考：**

> In order to fabricate gadgets, you'll need to shoot some resources in your Refinery.

> But remember: any resources deposited into a Refinery CANNOT be removed.

### 精炼链接（Refinery Links）

精炼链接可以便捷地将资源和结晶传送至精炼炉，以便在实验室的史莱姆科技中使用。

**原文参考：**

> Refinery links conveniently transport resources and plorts to the refinery where they can be used exclusively for slime science in the Slime Science Lab.

### 制造器（The Fabricator）

拿到设计图之后，就可以用制造器，将精炼炉中的资源制作成装置啦。来试试看！

**原文参考：**

> The Fabricator allows you to craft vac upgrades and gadgets using resources stored in the Refinery. Give it a try!

### 装置模式（Enter Gadget Mode）

有新的装置可用！快去装置模式看看吧！

**原文参考：**

> You have new gadgets available! Check them out in Gadget Mode!

### 放置点（Placing Gadgets）

在世界中选择一个位置，放置你的装置吧！

**原文参考：**

> Pick a spot to place your gadget in the world!

### 实验室（The Lab）

手头有富余的结晶和新币？那就得好好探索下史莱姆科技的新天地了！

去看一看温室下方的实验室，在那里你能升级真空枪和制造各种装置。

**原文参考：**

> Have some plorts and newbucks to spare? If so, you'll want to explore the wonders of slime science!

> Check out the Lab underneath the Conservatory to begin crafting vac upgrades and gadgets.

### 你设置了一个双向传送器！（You placed a two-way teleporter!）

双向传送器可以用来在两个地点之间创建一条自定义的传送路径。安装两个同套的传送器，来创建你心仪的传送路径。

**原文参考：**

> Two-way teleporters can be used to create a custom warp route between two locations. Install both teleporters from the same set to create your desired warp route.

### 免费装饰（Free Deco）

世界各地散落着的装饰品等你来发现！如果你正在查看某个物品，屏幕上出现了文字说明，可以尝试进入装置模式将其拾取。

**原文参考：**

> Some deco is left out in the world for you to find! If you're looking at something and text appears onscreen to identify it, try entering gadget mode to pick it up.

## 无人机教程（t.tut.*）

> 补充量子无人机机制（正文见 science/quantum_drones.md）：

### 新手无人机指南（Let's Get Buzzing）

量子无人机可自动化牧场运作，或部署在彩虹岛各处采集资源。进入装置模式放置无人机站点试试吧！

> Quantum Drones can automate your ranch or be placed elsewhere on Rainbow Island to acquire resources. Give it a try by placing a Drone Station in Gadget Mode!

### 无人机站点入门（Drone Station 101）

在无人机站点可以对无人机进行编程，设置它执行的任务。你也可以检查它的运行情况，补充燃料或是从量子云中取出。

> Your drone can be programmed at a Drone Station to set what tasks it performs. You can also check how it's doing, refuel it, or withdraw from the Quantum Cloud.

### 无人机任务（Drone Tasks）

设置好无人机的采集对象（目标）、采集位置（来源）以及采集物的送达地点（目的地）后，激活无人机即可开始工作。快来试试吧！

> Program your drone on what to collect (its Target), where it's looking (its Source), and where it takes things (its Destination). Once that's done, Activate Your Drone. Try it out!

### 无人机模式：牧场主模式或勘探模式（Drone Modes: Rancher or Explorer）

温室外的量子无人机会进入特殊的勘探模式！该模式下无人机可将结晶或资源传送到量子云端。

> Quantum Drones placed outside the Conservatory enter a special Explorer Mode! This mode allows drones to manifest plorts or resources and send them to the Quantum Cloud.

### 为无人机补充燃料（Fuel Your Drone）

随着时间的推移消耗能量的无人机，在能量耗尽时会进入休眠，向无人机站点燃料口发射结晶即可补充。试试看吧！

> Drones lose energy over time and will sleep when they run out. They can be refueled by shooting plorts into the Drone Station's fuel port. Try it out!

### 全局监控无人机！（Keep an eye on those drones!）

你可以在地图上看到你的无人机站点的位置和它们当前的能量水平！你还可以在这里窥视量子云中的内容——但请记住，你只能从无人机站点取出或发送资源！

> You can see where your Drone Stations are located and their current energy levels right on the Map! You can also peek at what’s in your Quantum Cloud here—but remember, you can only withdraw or send resources from a Drone Station!

### 我的设备都去哪儿了？（Where's all my stuff?）

勘探无人机通过扫描区域，发现<i>可能</i>存在的结晶或资源，并将其量子版本存储在量子云中。通过取出它们或者把它们送到市场或精炼炉来把它们变成现实！

> An Explorer drone scans its area to figure out what plorts or resources <i>could</i> exist there, then stores quantum versions of them in the Quantum Cloud. Turn them real by withdrawing them or sending them to the Market or Refinery!

### 神秘科技获取......？（Mysterious Technology Acquired...?）

你获得的量子部件正散发着近乎异界的能量波动。快前往实验室看看能打造什么！

> The Quantum Component you just acquired vibrates with an almost otherworldly energy. See what you can build with it in the Lab!

## 辉光史莱姆 / 圣域 / 彩晶教程

### 这只史莱姆简直光芒四射！（This Slime is Positively Radiant!）

恭喜你收集到第一只辉光史莱姆！它和普通史莱姆一样，只是更加光彩夺目。探索世界的时候要多留意......你可能会发现更多这样的史莱姆！

> Congratulations on collecting your first Radiant Slime! It's just like a normal slime, but more radiant. Keep an eye out as you explore the world... you might find more of them!

### 那东西刚才亮起来了吗？（Did That Thing Just Light Up?）

装饰圣域房间真的能让它焕然一新，是吧？在每个房间里添加各种各样的东西，可能会也可能不会发生一些奇妙的事情......这可真是个谜！不妨亲自试试吧。

> Decorating a Sanctuary room can really liven it up, right? Add a variety of things in each room and something cool might happen... or it might not. It's a real mystery! Try it for yourself.

### 切勿食用这种彩晶（Don't Eat THESE Sprinkles）

史莱姆（尤其是辉光史莱姆）会在它们的圣域拓展区域中留下一些彩晶，这些彩晶会用收集罐收集起来！你可以在圣域商店用这些彩晶购买特殊商品。快来试试吧！

> Slimes, especially Radiant Slimes, leave sprinkles behind in their Sanctuary expansions, which the canisters then collect! Sprinkles can be deposited at the Sanctuary's shop to purchase special products. Try it out!
