## 习性
迷宫梦幻之地的粉色蓬松岛屿间栖息着昏昏史莱姆，它们在自身散发出的奇怪气息中时睡时醒。起初，它们看起来像是有些懒惰的猎手，当它们碰巧醒着的时候，就会美美地吃上一口鸡肉。然而，考虑到梦幻之地在迷宫中的作用，昏昏史莱姆可能是一种更为强大的生物。甚至可能当它们在云间酣睡时，它们可以将周围的世界从梦境拉入现实世界。

**原文参考：**

> Sloomber slimes are found among the puffy, pink islands of the Labyrinth’s Dream Land, drifting in and out of sleep from the strange auras that radiate from them. At first they seem like somewhat lazy hunters, delighting in a chomp of chicken when they happen to be awake. However, given the function of Dream Land within the Labyrinth, it is possible that sloomber slimes are significantly more powerful creatures, perhaps even dreaming the world around them into existence as they sleep among the clouds.

## 注意事项
昏昏史莱姆散发出的奇怪气息可以让其他史莱姆，包括其他昏昏史莱姆，进入一段时间的睡眠状态。如果你想要休息一下，这可能会帮上你的忙，但它可能会打乱你牧场的喂食计划和结晶生产。所以一定要时刻了解它们在你牧场的什么位置，因为据说它们的睡眠气息无法......感觉到......直到......已经......太......晚了......

**原文参考：**

> The strange aura that emanates from sloomber slimes can put other slimes, including other sloomber slimes, to sleep for some time. While this can be a good thing if you’re looking to take a break, it can disrupt feeding schedules and plort production on your ranch. So be sure to always know where they are on your ranch because it is said that their sleep aura can’t be… felt… until…… it’s…….. too…………

## 结晶说明
如果不佩戴手套等安全设备，握持含有浓缩昏昏精华的昏昏结晶会让你陷入奇幻异梦、深沉舒适的睡眠。一旦醒来，你会完全回忆起梦境中的每一个细节，并持续一整天都感觉恬淡宁静、精神焕发。因此，昏昏结晶在任何情况下都不应在戴手套的情况下握持。

**原文参考：**

> If held without safety equipment like a pair of gloves, the concentrated sloomber essence within a sloomber plort can make you fall into a deep, cozy sleep full of wonderful dreams. Once you finally awake you will recall every bit of the dream to its fullest, and feel a sense of deep calm and refreshment that lasts all day. Therefore, a sloomber plort should never under any circumstances be held while wearing gloves.
