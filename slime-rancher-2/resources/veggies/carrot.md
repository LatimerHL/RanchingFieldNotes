## 关于
其貌不扬的胡萝卜种植简单又富有营养，是最适合初学者种植的作物。早期开拓者们在致远牧星上到处都种满了胡萝卜，因为它们是大家都喜欢的靠谱零食——大家是指史莱姆和它们的人类小伙伴。

老话说得好：聪明的牧场主会将胡萝卜储备得满满的，满到就像在吃蔬菜自助的史莱姆的餐盘。

**原文参考：**

> Easy-growing and nutritious, the humble carrot makes a perfect first crop for a new ranch. Early explorers planted them everywhere on the Far, Far Range because they're a dependable snack that everyone enjoys - slimes and their human companions alike. 

> As the old adage goes: a good rancher keeps their carrot reserves as full as a slime at a veggie buffet.

## 在牧场

**原文参考：**

> Deposit a carrot into a garden's depositor and you'll have a large carrot crop of your very own.
