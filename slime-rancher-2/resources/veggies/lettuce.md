## 关于
像沙漠绿洲或深海珍珠一样，水甘蓝就是一个小小的奇迹。致远牧星的旱季对野生生物来说很难熬。但大自然也提供了一种宝贵的资源，那就是水甘蓝。

储存在植物内部的水分可以防止叶片在烈日阳光下枯萎；而卷曲的叶片则能保持水分清凉。对于被酷暑搞得口干舌燥的生物来说，简直再完美不过了。

**原文参考：**

> Like a desert bloom or a deep sea pearl, water lettuce is a small miracle. The dry season can be difficult for wildlife on the Far, Far Range. But through the humble water lettuce, nature has found a way to provide.

> The water stored inside the plant prevents its leaves from wilting under the sun's rays, and in turn the crisp leaves keep the water cool and protected, perfect for a thirsty critter in search of relief from the heat.

**原文参考：**

> Deposit a water lettuce into a garden's depositor and you'll have a large water lettuce crop of your very own.
