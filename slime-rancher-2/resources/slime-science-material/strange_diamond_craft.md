## 关于
就算在一片漆黑之中，奇异钻石也会如同被光照射一般，发出璀璨的光芒。奇异钻石由于其在传送科技中的重要作用，价值极高。奇异钻石密度高到不可思议，构成也十分复杂。它可以在自身内部对光线进行完全折射，因此它也能扭曲时间。

奇艺钻石经常发现于洞穴或其他黑暗的地点，仿佛大自然想偷偷独占奇艺钻石的璀璨一般。

**原文参考：**

> Even in the dark, these diamonds seem to shine as if light is passing through them. Strange diamonds are highly valued for their use in a variety of warp tech processes. Being almost impossibly dense and intricate in their composition, a strange diamond can reflect light within itself so profoundly that it actually bends time.

> Strange diamonds are most often found in caves and other dark places, as if the natural world wants to keep their brilliance all to itself.

## 在牧场
将奇异钻石放入实验室的精炼炉，就可以用它制作史莱姆科技装置或升级真空枪了。

**原文参考：**

> Deposit a strange diamond into the Lab's Refinery where it can then be used for crafting Vac Upgrades or Slime Science gadgets.
