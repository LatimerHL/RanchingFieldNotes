## 关于
极度炫目的雷击后，闪电微粒就会出现。尽管它会因为残余能量而发出噼啪声，但对其进行收集、处理实际上是安全的。

**原文参考：**

> Lightning motes are left behind after especially dazzling lightning strikes. Though crackling with residual energy, they are in fact safe for collection and handling.

## 在牧场
将闪电微粒放入实验室的精炼炉，就可以用它制作史莱姆科技装置或升级真空枪了。

**原文参考：**

> Deposit a lightning mote into the Lab's Refinery where it can then be used for crafting Vac Upgrades or Slime Science gadgets.
