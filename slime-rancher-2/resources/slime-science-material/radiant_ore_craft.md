## 关于
在彩虹岛众多的怪奇事物中，辉光矿石应该算是最引人注目的。在激光温度计下，它的温度读数超过100摄氏度，但它摸起来却很凉，而且融化后会变成冰冷的液体。

然而一旦在显微镜下观察，辉光矿石色彩变化背后的机理就很容易理解了：它是由极小的纯净彩虹沉积物构成的。

辉光矿石会轻轻振动并发出轻微的嗡嗡声，仿佛正在散发出某种不寻常的宇宙能量。有点瘆人呢。

**原文参考：**

> Of all of Rainbow Island's many oddities, radiant ore may be the most remarkable. Under a laser thermometer, its temperature reads over 100 degrees celsius, but it is cool to the touch and becomes an icy liquid when melted.

> The mechanics behind its shifting array of pastel colors is easily understood once observed under a microscope: it's made up of infinitesimal deposits of pure rainbow.

> Then there's the way it gently vibrates, humming, as if radiating some strange cosmic energy. Spooky.
