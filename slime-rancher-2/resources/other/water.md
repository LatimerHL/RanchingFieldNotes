## 概况

## 关于
水是极其有用的资源，可以在致远牧星各地找到。和史莱姆之海的水不同，水池和瀑布的水可以用真空枪吸起并用水槽升级便捷储存。储存起来的水可以从真空枪射出，创造许多有益的效果。

**原文参考：**

> Water is an extremely useful resource found across the Far, Far Range. Unlike water in the slime sea, water from pools and waterfalls can be suctioned into the vac pack and stored using the handy Water Tank upgrade. Once stored, it can be shot out of the vac at the world around you, creating numerous beneficial effects.

## 在牧场
水可以用来加速园地里作物的生长，让史莱姆冷静下来，并在烦人的焦油怪吞噬你的史莱姆前驱散它们。水甚至可以冲掉水晶史莱姆的晶体结构或在炸弹史莱姆爆炸之前让它们稳定下来。对任何牧场主来说，水都是美妙无比的道具，但要记得可以找到水的地点：水资源不多，并且非常分散。

**原文参考：**

> Water can be used to speed up garden growth, calm down slimes, and disperse pesky Tarr before they gobble up your slimes. Water can even wash away Crystal slimes' crystal formations or neutralize Boom slimes before they explode. Water is truly a wondrous tool for any rancher, but remember where you found it: water sources are few and far between.
