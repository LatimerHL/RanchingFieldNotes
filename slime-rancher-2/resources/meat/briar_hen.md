## 关于
母石楠鸡是致远牧星上荒野灌木中特产的变种鸡。很久以前有个科学家发现，它们有着复杂的部落结构，还有十分神奇的石楠鸡习俗。探险家差点就加入石楠鸡部落，成为终身荣誉鸡呢！

但他的记录嘎然而止，我们对于这位探险家和他的发现也止步于此。哦对了，我们倒是知道，他的名字叫卡尔。

**原文参考：**

> Briar hens are a chicken variant born from the wild thickets of the Far, Far Range. They were first discovered long ago by an explorer who noted that they seemed to have a complex tribal structure, and a fascinating series of chicken customs. He even may have joined the briar tribe as an honorary chicken himself!

> His records end there however, and we know nothing else about him or his findings. Oh, but his name was Carl. We know that.

## 在牧场
公鸡周围的母石楠鸡每隔一段时间就会下蛋，并孵出石楠鸡仔儿。然而，如果鸡群密度过大，就会导致它们感到焦虑，停止产蛋。能够理解鸡与鸡之间恋爱结合的复杂过程的聪明牧场主们，向来都会监控鸡圈中的生长率，并在鸡群生长减速时降低鸡群密度。

**原文参考：**

> Briar hens in close proximity to roostros will periodically lay eggs that produce briar chicks. However, keeping too many chickens in close proximity makes them anxious and egg production will come to a halt. Savvy ranchers with an understanding of the complex nature of chicken romance will monitor growth rates in their coops and reduce density when growth begins to slow.
