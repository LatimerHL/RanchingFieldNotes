## 关于
五颜六色的彩色母鸡曾经只能在致远牧星的边境附近见到，那里被称为玻璃荒漠。

鉴于彩虹岛的植物群全都光彩夺目，而这正好与彩色母鸡的翅膀一模一样，难道这些鸟真的起源于彩虹岛？如果是这样，他们又是如何到达几乎不可能到达的玻璃沙漠的呢？

这一切构成了我们宇宙中另一个不可思议的鸡类谜团。

**原文参考：**

> Painted hens are colorful chickens once found exclusively in a very distant region of the Far, Far Range known as the Glass Desert.

> Given that the prismatic qualities of its flora match the plumage of a painted hen, could it be that these birds actually originated on Rainbow Island? If so, how did they also manage to get to the nearly impossible to reach Glass Desert? 

> It all adds up to yet another incredible, chicken-related mystery in our universe.

## 在牧场
公鸡周围的彩色母鸡每隔一段时间就会下蛋，并孵出彩色鸡仔儿。然而，如果鸡群密度过大，就会导致它们感到焦虑，停止产蛋。能够理解鸡与鸡之间恋爱结合的复杂过程的聪明牧场主们，向来都会监控鸡圈中的生长率，并在鸡群生长减速时降低鸡群密度。

**原文参考：**

> Painted hens in close proximity to roostros will periodically lay eggs that produce painted chicks. However, keeping too many chickens in close proximity makes them anxious and egg production will come to a halt. Savvy ranchers with an understanding of the complex nature of chicken romance will monitor growth rates in their coops and reduce density when growth begins to slow.
