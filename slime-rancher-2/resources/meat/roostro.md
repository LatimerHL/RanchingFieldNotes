## 关于
公鸡是致远牧星上最早一批鸟类，也是牧场上孵养小鸡的重要因素。然而，这些公鸡比任何母鸡都要稀有，因此找到它们的时候可不要放过哦。

要想获得公鸡有两种途径，一是在致远牧星上捕捉，二是自己在牧场进行孵化。任何种类的鸡仔儿都有较小的几率成长为公鸡。有些牧场主认为，如果每天日出时对正在打鸣的公鸡王虔诚致敬的话，这个几率就会升高。

**原文参考：**

> Roostros are the alpha birds on the Far, Far Range and half of the equation required to breed chickens on the ranch. However, roostros are rarer than any hen making them a great find any time one is discovered.

> To acquire a roostro, either find one out on the range or breed them on the ranch. Any type of chickadoo has a small chance of growing into a roostro. Some ranchers believe that these odds increase if you greet the Rooster King's crow at sunrise with an enthusiastic 'good morning!'

## 在牧场
公鸡周围的各种母鸡每隔一段时间就会下蛋，并孵出鸡仔儿。然而，如果鸡群密度过大，就会导致它们感到焦虑，停止产蛋。能够理解鸡与鸡之间恋爱结合的复杂过程的聪明牧场主们，向来都会监控鸡圈中的生长率，并在鸡群生长减速时降低鸡群密度。

**原文参考：**

> All hens in close proximity to roostros will periodically lay eggs that produce chickadoos. However, keeping too many chickens in close proximity makes them anxious and egg production will come to a halt. Savvy ranchers with an understanding of the complex nature of chicken romance will monitor growth rates in their coops and reduce density when growth begins to slow.
