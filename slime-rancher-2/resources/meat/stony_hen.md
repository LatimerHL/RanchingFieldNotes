## 关于
目前普遍认为，母岩鸡是由早期跋涉到致远牧星深山中的非常母鸡进化而来。也许当时那些母鸡认为，只有在那险峻荒芜的峭壁之上冥想，才能找寻自我的真谛吧。好了不逗了，它们应该只是傻乎乎走丢了。

**原文参考：**

> Stony hens are thought to have evolved from hen hens who once trekked deep into the mountains of the Far, Far Range. Perhaps these birds were seeking a clarity of mind that one can only truly obtain through meditation amongst those lofty, barren crags. But most likely, they were just stupid and got lost.

## 在牧场
公鸡周围的母岩鸡每隔一段时间就会下蛋，并孵出岩鸡仔儿。然而，如果鸡群密度过大，就会导致它们感到焦虑，停止产蛋。能够理解鸡与鸡之间恋爱结合的复杂过程的聪明牧场主们，向来都会监控鸡圈中的生长率，并在鸡群生长减速时降低鸡群密度。

**原文参考：**

> Stony hens in close proximity to roostros will periodically lay eggs that produce stony chicks. However, keeping too many chickens in close proximity makes them anxious and egg production will come to a halt. Savvy ranchers with an understanding of the complex nature of chicken romance will monitor growth rates in their coops and reduce density when growth begins to slow.
