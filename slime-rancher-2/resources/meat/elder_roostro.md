## 关于
老公鸡是已经从生儿育女岗位上退休的老年公鸡。“年轻的公鸡都太爱瞎叫了。而且走路也太快，而且对我们这种老同志根本不够尊重嘛。”这是老公鸡对年青一代的普遍评价，一般说这话的时候还要恶心地吧唧嘴。

不过，考虑到鸡根本没有嘴唇，吧唧嘴应该是不可能的才对吧。

**原文参考：**

> Elder roostros are the senior-aged roostros who have retired from the otherwise bustling life of their younger counterparts. 'Young roostros crow too much, they move too fast, and frankly have no respect for their elders,' an elder roostro might say of youths; as they make a gross, smacking sound with their lips.

> Though this is of course impossible, as roostros do not have lips.

## 在牧场
过气的老公鸡已经无法生育鸡仔儿，除了变成史莱姆口中肉质较老的鸡肉大餐之外，没有什么别的用处。

**原文参考：**

> An elder roostro can no longer sire chickadoos and serves no other purpose than becoming a slightly tough chicken dinner.
