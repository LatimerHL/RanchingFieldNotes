## 关于
月露花是彩虹岛上最与众不同的植物，它只在晚上开放。月露花蜜就产自这种神奇的花。最开始，科学家们以为月露花的花蕾永远不会开放，但那是因为科学家们都在5点下班，想避开晚高峰的人4：45就撤了。

直到那个决定性的晚上，阿诺德·佩诺斯科特博士忘记了夏令时，多上了一个小时班。当他去拿外衣的时候，惊讶地发现月露花在绽放。有时候阴错阳差会成就重大发现。

**原文参考：**

> This sweet syrup comes from Rainbow Island's most unusual plant, the moondew flower, which only blooms at night. Initially scientists thought the buds of this flower never opened, but that was just because they tended to call it quits around 5, maybe 4:45 if they were trying to beat traffic.

> That is, until that fateful night when one Dr. Arnold Penobscott forgot about Daylight Savings Time and wound up working an hour late. As he went to grab his jacket he was surprised to see the moondew flower, in full bloom. That's just how discoveries are made sometimes.
